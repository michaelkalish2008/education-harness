# Calibration rules (used by welcome.md and planning)

- Treat a project title or `focus` as a topic, not proof of a learning goal or prior knowledge.
- Ask in everyday words, one question a turn, each with "Not sure" and "Skip". Never show the internal labels below to the learner.
- Ask up front only what changes the first lesson: what it's for (`learning_context`) and its one follow-up (`school_level`, `target_role` or `professional_context`). Curiosity needs no follow-up.
- Ask the rest only when it matters, in plain words, and save each through `profile` on the learner's yes:
  - `sociability` ("Is anyone learning with you?"): ask early for school, or when a parent, teacher, tutor or group comes up, since it decides who hears how they're doing. Values: `group`, `guided`, `solo`.
  - `lesson_mode` ("Want a full plan, a few steps at a time, or just follow your questions?"): ask when the first plan is written. Values: `structured`, `semi-structured`, `free-form`.
  - `mastery_mode` ("When you're ready to show what you know, would you rather write and do, talk it through, or both?"): ask the first time a step is drafted. Values: `written-practical`, `spoken`, `both`.
  - `depth_mode`: propose it from the first lesson's work (welcome.md, Turn 3); never ask it cold. Values: `civic-overview`, `foundational`, `practitioner`, `advanced`. Civic overview is complete, not remedial.
- Infer formats from the device and from requests like "show me another way". Don't ask how they learn best; self-report is weak evidence. Keep an inference unsaved until the learner confirms it.
- Read `standards.matches` before planning quantitative material. Inspect statements, not codes alone. Use relevant standards to identify prerequisites worth checking; ignore irrelevant matches and never let standards determine scope.
- Do not infer calculus or matrix notation comfort from one technically correct remark. Start below the first unconfirmed prerequisite, with plain language and a tiny example. Introduce symbols only after naming what each symbol means, then check that the representation works for the learner.
- Placement on the topic itself (what they can already do with it) comes before the first explanation: `../../tutor/references/placement.md`.
- Self-reported level and observed work can disagree. Follow the work, offer "Simpler · Just right · Go further" when they stall or breeze through, and propose a change in one line before saving it.
